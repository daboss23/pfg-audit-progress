Executive summary

A better website. A better first impression. More opportunities for new clients.

PFG has built a respected financial planning business over 36 years, but its current website doesn't fully reflect that experience or make it as easy as it could be for potential clients to take the next step.

Our new demonstration shows how that could change.

We've redesigned the experience to better showcase PFG's services, build trust and make it easier for visitors to find information, explore their options and book an appointment.

The result is a clearer, more professional website concept designed to help turn more online visitors into genuine business opportunities.

1. What we've demonstrated

A more professional design, clearer service information, easier navigation, a more accessible appointment-booking experience and new ways to engage potential clients.

2. Ready to personalise

The demonstration includes sections for client reviews, adviser profiles, educational articles and videos that can be completed with PFG's genuine information and approved content.

3. What would come next

If PFG chooses to proceed, the next stage would involve reviewing the concept together, completing the remaining functionality and connecting the necessary business systems.

How the demo is designed

Conversion-focused design & functionality

Beyond improving the appearance of the website, we've applied proven direct-response marketing principles to how visitors experience and navigate the site. Every key page has been designed with a purpose: to build trust, clearly communicate PFG's value and guide potential clients towards taking action.

Through stronger messaging, strategically placed enquiry buttons, easier appointment booking and a smoother journey between pages, the website is designed to reduce unnecessary steps and create more opportunities for a prospect to become a lead.

The goal is simple: turn more website visitors into genuine conversations, qualified leads and ultimately, new clients for PFG.

If PFG chooses to proceed

The proposed website provides a starting point for what PFG's future online experience could look like.

If the direction is a good fit, the next step would be to review the demonstration together, confirm the features PFG would like to use and agree on the requirements for a live website.

PFG already uses Calendly for adviser bookings, so the existing booking system could potentially be reused rather than replaced.

Other connections, including business email, website tracking, approved content and enquiry management, could be completed during implementation.

No live website changes or account access are required to review the demonstration.

  • BookingReuse PFG's existing Calendly accounts, so bookings land in each adviser's diary and confirmations are sent.
  • EnquiriesRoute every booking and enquiry to PFG's inbox or CRM, so the one-business-day reply promise can be met.
  • NewsletterConnect the sign-up to PFG's email platform.
  • Ask PFG assistantAgree its answers through PFG's approval process with Akumin, and route anything it can't answer to client services.

The next opportunity

Search is changing. PFG has an opportunity to get ahead.

People are no longer relying solely on Google to research financial advice. They're increasingly using AI-powered tools such as ChatGPT and Google's AI search features to understand their options, compare services and find answers to financial questions.

This creates an additional opportunity for PFG.

By combining traditional search optimisation with AI Search Optimisation, we could make PFG's website easier to discover, better equipped to answer potential clients' questions and more useful to people researching financial planning services.

1. Get found on Google

Improve how PFG appears in relevant searches by strengthening service pages, adviser profiles, local business information and website content.

2. Prepare for AI-powered search

Create clear, expert-led content that search engines and AI systems can understand and potentially reference when answering questions about financial planning, retirement and superannuation.

3. Turn interest into enquiries

Connect helpful content with clear next steps, making it easier for visitors to explore PFG's services, enquire or book an appointment.

The goal isn't simply more website traffic. It's helping the right people discover PFG, build confidence in its expertise and take the next step towards becoming a client.

Proposed future improvements

If PFG chooses to pursue this strategy, the work could include the steps below. Good traditional search optimisation remains the foundation for being found in AI-powered search, so most of this work helps both.

  1. Optimising the existing Google Business Profiles for Melbourne and Brisbane.
  2. Creating useful, location-specific financial planning pages.
  3. Publishing genuine adviser-reviewed guides about retirement, superannuation, fees and other common financial questions.
  4. Adding verified adviser qualifications, credentials and appropriate business information.
  5. Improving search indexing, website structure and crawl accessibility.
  6. Adding accurate structured data where useful.
  7. Monitoring search performance and enquiries through Google Search Console and Analytics.
  8. Reviewing AI search visibility and citations where measurable.

This is a proposed future opportunity, separate from the checklist score above. No search engine or AI tool can be guaranteed to rank, recommend or cite a website, and any financial information published would be accurate, appropriately qualified and reviewed through PFG's approval process.

Looking further ahead

Future opportunities in AI-powered advertising

As AI search and advertising platforms develop, new ways of reaching potential clients may become available. PFG could explore these opportunities as platform eligibility and financial advertising regulations evolve.

Not part of the demonstrated website or this proposal. Under OpenAI's current advertising policies, financial services ads, including financial planning, are generally not permitted outside the United States, so ChatGPT advertising isn't an option for PFG today.
