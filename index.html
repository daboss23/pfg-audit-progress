Executive summary

The leaks a prospect would notice are closed.

The October audit found a 36-year firm behind a website that worked like a digital business card. The new site turns it into one that wins enquiries: a booking calendar behind every button, clear fees, a "what to expect" guide for the first meeting, a free personalised Retirement Wealth Blueprint, and an after-hours assistant. The demo post, the "Coming soon" page, the "Contact Form Demo" heading, the AMP privacy note and Angelo's link to Adam's calendar are all gone.

We scored it strictly. Where the new site shows sample content waiting for PFG's real material (reviews, articles, Angelo's video), we count it as partly met, not met. That puts the new site at 65 today. It reaches 75 once we finish the remaining build work, 93 once PFG's reviews, videos, articles and adviser details are in, and 100 once analytics and directory listings are set up.

1. Fixed

Every clean-up item from the audit, booking on every page, fees, the first-meeting guide, the Blueprint calculator, the assistant, search descriptions and alt text. Converting visitors goes from 38% to 100%.

2. Ready for PFG's content

Sections are built and waiting for real material: Google reviews, articles, adviser videos, qualifications and register links, and the year of the Beddoes award.

3. Still to do

A sitemap, a few dead links, location pages, security headers, and the parts that need PFG's own accounts: analytics, Google Business Profiles and directory listings.

Before the new site goes live

The new site is a working preview. These connections keep its "meets" scores true once real clients use it.

  • Booking calendarConnect to each adviser's real calendar, so bookings land in their diaries and confirmations are sent.
  • EnquiriesSend every booking and enquiry to PFG's inbox or CRM, so the one-business-day reply promise is met.
  • NewsletterConnect the sign-up to PFG's email platform.
  • Ask PFG assistantAgree its answers with Akumin, and route anything it can't answer to client services.
  • Remove the preview noticeTake the concept demo line out of the footer and point pfg.com.au at the new site.
  • Keep old addresses workingRedirect pfg.com.au's old page addresses to their new pages, so Google rankings carry over.
